01 / About

Low-level engineering.
Product-level thinking.

A hands-on engineering practice for teams working at the intersection of Linux software, silicon and physical devices.

Engineering focus

Make the platform stable, diagnosable and ready for the demands of the product.

I am an Embedded Linux engineer focused on platform software and hardware–software integration. My work spans Linux kernel customization, board support packages, device-driver development, boot flows and embedded Linux image construction.

I approach problems from the hardware boundary upward: understand the board and boot chain, inspect kernel and device-tree behavior, isolate the failure, implement the smallest correct change, and validate it on the target platform.

Through Nexembed Innovation, I am interested in helping teams turn embedded product requirements into robust, maintainable implementations.

04 / Working approach

Understand. Isolate. Implement. Verify.

A disciplined workflow keeps low-level changes traceable and reduces avoidable regressions.

STEP 01

Scope the system

Clarify the board, SoC, boot chain, kernel baseline, interfaces and acceptance criteria.

STEP 02

Reproduce & diagnose

Collect serial logs, inspect configuration and device tree, and narrow the fault boundary.

STEP 03

Implement deliberately

Make focused changes with clear dependencies, reviewable patches and engineering notes.

STEP 04

Validate on target

Verify expected behavior on hardware and run relevant regression checks before handover.
